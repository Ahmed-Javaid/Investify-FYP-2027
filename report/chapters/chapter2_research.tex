\chapter{Research and Technical Background}
\section{Financial Text and Prediction}
FinBERT adapts language representations to financial sentiment and provides positive, neutral and negative scores \cite{finbert_paper,finbert_card}. Investify derives news polarity from those scores and combines it with technical indicators. The language model is an input component, not a stock-return oracle. FNSPID supplies historical financial text \cite{fnspid_paper}; source coverage and timestamp precision must be audited before aligning it to tradable sessions.
\section{Model Selection by Text Register}
Financial headlines and informal social posts differ in language and context. FinBERT remains the news model. CryptoBERT was proposed for social content, but its crypto-domain training does not establish equity-tweet transfer \cite{cryptobert_card}. Cardiff's Twitter sentiment model provides a relevant alternative with explicit mention/URL preprocessing \cite{twitter_sentiment_card}. Both original checkpoints were evaluated on the same frozen references. Cardiff's exploratory lead and the added sanity probes are reported in Chapter~\ref{chap:testing}; changing the social candidate is documented as an evaluated alternative to the proposal, not a replacement for FinBERT.
\section{Attribution and Explanation Checking}
TreeSHAP decomposes a tree model's raw score into a base value and feature contributions \cite{treeshap_paper}. These values are not calibrated probabilities or economic causes. Explanation research distinguishes faithfulness from self-consistency \cite{faithfulness_paper}; Investify therefore makes a narrow, testable claim: supported statements must match the selected record. FActScore motivates atomic-claim accounting \cite{factscore_2023}, while RARR illustrates evidence-based correction \cite{rarr_2023}. Our contribution is a bounded implementation with visible-text/JSON independence, coverage gates, retained rejection and independently checked fallback.

\section{Multiclass Calibration}
Independent one-versus-rest isotonic maps need not preserve the multiclass ranking. The fresh maps make Buy's minimum exceed the other classes' maxima, creating constant output. Temperature scaling instead divides all class margins by one positive parameter and normalizes jointly \cite{guo_temperature_2017}. We fit that parameter on earlier calibration outcomes only. The observed fit approaches uniform confidence, so the interface abstains rather than treating a preserved raw class as a usable recommendation. This is a post-analysis correction. Parameters use calibration outcomes only; evaluation cases were already examined, so the new score is diagnostic rather than a first-unseen performance claim.
\section{Risk Capacity and Tolerance}
Capacity describes the ability to bear loss; tolerance describes willingness. Grable--Lytton uses thirteen items with a published scoring key and range 13--47 \cite{grable_retrospective_2015}. We retain the scoring and display concise paraphrases, alongside five capacity factors and explicit protective caps. The more restrictive capacity/tolerance tier binds. A normalized allowance controls required confidence; it is no longer a sentiment weight. Local questionnaire wording and the project confidence policy still require population-specific evaluation.
\section{Market Mechanisms and Data Vintage}
Stocks represent ownership interests and may respond to company results, financing conditions, expectations and market-wide events \cite{sec_stocks}. Technical indicators describe recent price/volume structure; sentiment describes the text, not its truth. SHAP describes how a fitted model used an input, not why the market moved.

NVIDIA's four-for-one split began split-adjusted trading on 20 July 2021 \cite{nvda_split_2021}; the ten-for-one split began on 10 June 2024 \cite{nvda_split_2024}. The second event occurs after our 2018--2023 window, but a 2026 vendor snapshot can retroactively rescale earlier prices and volumes. A split changes share count and per-share scale, not holding value by itself. We state the consumed SIP split-adjusted basis and its acquisition vintage rather than describing it as original as-traded data. Dividends, action metadata and provider revisions remain distinct audit questions.

Exchange holidays and early closes require a declared timing policy \cite{nyse_calendar}. The inherited baseline uses a fixed 16:00 New York cutoff, including half-days; it is not claimed as actual-close execution. Forward labels mature after five observed sessions. Label-end purging and the additional configured calendar-day gap are separate controls, explained in Chapter~6.
\section{Teacher and Student Choice}
The initial open-weight preference reflected anticipated restrictions on broad model distillation. For the current scoped construction pilot, a frontier assistant supplies explanatory examples; no competing general-purpose model or student adaptation is trained. The service restriction concerns competing models rather than a blanket prohibition on every educational example \cite{openai_terms_2026}. Phi-3-mini remains the planned original student and Mistral a fallback. The larger teacher/student comparison remains a later milestone. The construction method and development assistance are disclosed once in Appendix~\ref{app:handbook}.
\section{Research Gaps}
Useful prediction, unrestricted explanation quality, news/social divergence, multi-company generalization, population-specific risk interpretation and participant benefit remain open. The report separates those research questions from verified implementation behavior. Negative findings preserve their value as diagnostic evidence and guide the dated development plan.
