\section{Technical Completion and Team Submission Todo}
The team defines technical Mid-1 completion by working code, a complete authored report, working CI/CD and the required first-mid modules. Printing/signatures, supervisor review, member walkthroughs and rehearsal are separate team tasks and are excluded from this completion percentage. The earlier weighted readiness estimate combined these different activities and remains historical planning evidence.

The team todo is to review the report and sources, print and sign the required pages, complete individual code walkthroughs, rehearse the shared demonstration and check the projector. Supervisor scope/design and proposal-action review remains a genuine team responsibility. Applicable data/model-use and participant permissions are obtained before activities that need them. No review, signature, student authorship or research quality is inferred from technical completion.
