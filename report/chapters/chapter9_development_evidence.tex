\section{Current Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The prediction study covers NVDA and one historical interval. Broader companies, regimes and continuous-market generalization are unmeasured.
\item Source timing rules are implemented, but historical vendor availability and a byte-identical original acquisition are unproven. The fixed 16:00 cutoff still needs early-close reconciliation; cash dividends are not adjusted.
\item Clean prediction is below stratified random. Isotonic collapses classes; temperature reaches 1000 and approximately uniform confidence. No useful forecasting or return claim follows.
\item Risk is a post-hoc confidence gate, not a fitted personalization feature. All real temperature cases remain Observe. Local questionnaire wording and suitability have not been population-validated.
\item Social signals are not integrated into NVDA features. The frozen 24-post MSFT comparison uses a single AI reference pass; human validity and kappa are unavailable.
\item The implemented explainer is deterministic. The verifier recognizes template grammar, not unrestricted semantic/causal claims. Six teacher examples do not establish a trained or useful student.
\item Runtime NFRs, production hosting, live/backtest parity, independent installation and participant comprehension are not yet measured in the proposed environments.
\item Submission date/session, signatures and formal meeting records have not been supplied. The corresponding fields remain blank; no institutional approval is inferred.
\end{enumerate}
