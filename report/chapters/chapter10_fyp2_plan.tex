\chapter{Remaining Development Plan}
\section{Remaining Scope}
The full product requires audited multi-company inputs, news/social divergence, improved prediction/calibration, population-specific profile interpretation, evaluated generated explanations, serving/deployment and participant acceptance. QLoRA is a later milestone; brokerage execution remains excluded.
\section{Integration and Evaluation}
The next analysis compares declared baselines and development-only model/calibration choices. We retain weak findings rather than selecting on held-out scores. Social deployment selection follows agreed adequacy criteria; the divergence contribution requires a predeclared ablation on integrated data. Student work begins with a verified original checkpoint, a scored prompted baseline and a larger filtered training set.
\section{Operational Validation}
Measure target latency, memory and throughput; run live/backtest parity with a pinned clock; inject unavailable/thin-history sources; and document degraded responses. Production hosting and external installation require an explicit target and reproducible service package. Hosted artifact delivery is already implemented and remains distinct from hosting.
\section{Participant Evaluation}
Later tasks ask participants to interpret a recommendation, its drivers, uncertainty and verifier report. Record completion, time, assistance and interpretation errors with consent. The published risk instrument motivates scoring; local wording and policy suitability still need review. Tool/fixture success is not participant acceptance.
