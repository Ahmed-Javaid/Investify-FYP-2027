\chapter{Conclusion}
Investify has a substantive historical pipeline and a tested explanation increment. Complete technical/news evidence display, independent prose/JSON checking, hard contradiction rejection and verified fallback improve the proposal-stage demonstration. The local LLM adapter and frozen pilot protocol are implemented with explicit no-model behaviour, exact maintained definitions and fair deterministic comparators. They do not establish the quality or usefulness of an unconnected model.

The preserved historical evidence remains separate from completed fresh runs. A real Alpaca experiment executes all six leakage arms at both horizons, both corrected calibration modes and retained-model/native-TreeSHAP replay. The deliberately centred canary fires, but the clean five-day score is below its random comparator and both calibrated modes remain all Buy. Lower Brier than the raw model does not establish useful decisions or superiority to a simple class-prior forecast.

Original CryptoBERT and Cardiff each complete 24 held-out MSFT posts, with macro-F1 agreement 0.2564 and 0.6016 against frozen AI references. Cardiff is the exploratory benchmark leader; the small reference, two negative examples, public pretraining overlap and lack of independent human validation prevent a broad accuracy or deployment claim. The recorded Social Study viewer validates both raw output/receipt bundles. Final selection criteria remain unset.

Continuation should complete institutional report/deck inspection, actual metadata and signatures, member contributions and genuine review/rehearsal, while preserving weak findings and original evidence. Live LLM usefulness, student adaptation, broader generalisation, risk suitability and deployment remain unmeasured. No historical ownership or participant evidence is manufactured.

The current assistant teacher construction pilot records six actual verified explanations with four training and two validation examples; the planned Phi-3-mini student remains untrained. Exact teacher serving revision is unavailable, factual JSON supervision is assembled from original records, and no fine-tuning efficacy or human-understanding result is claimed.
