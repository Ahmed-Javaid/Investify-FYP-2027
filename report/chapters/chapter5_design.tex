\section{Architecture Decisions and Rationale}
Saved historical outputs keep the demonstration reproducible. A strict prior-session join makes the timing rule inspectable. Label endpoints and calibration availability prevent future outcomes from entering fit stages. The profile is a separate gate so it cannot reverse the recorded market class. The verifier is deterministic so an LLM does not grade its own claims. Rejection and fallback have separate reports so a successful replacement cannot conceal the original error.
\subsection{Explanation Layer (LLM) Design}
\label{sec:llm-design}
\textbf{Inputs:} company/session/horizon, class probabilities, all eleven technical/news values, selected-class SHAP values and ranks, maintained feature definitions, missing-data flags and the trusted risk-policy outcome. Future integrated records may add news/social divergence; absent social evidence must remain absent.

\textbf{Outputs:} plain-language prose plus a structured claims payload with record keys, claim types and the corresponding prose spans. The LLM must explain technical and sentiment signals, opposing contributions, uncertainty and what the data does not show. It cannot choose a new class, change the policy outcome or add a market fact not supported by the record.

\textbf{Permitted claims:} recorded values/probabilities, maintained indicator meanings, SHAP support/opposition for the selected raw class, missing coverage and the policy outcome. \textbf{Forbidden claims:} guaranteed returns, causal assertions from SHAP alone, confidence as profit, invented headline/social content, personal suitability or trades being executed.

\textbf{Verification and fallback:} check numbers, directions, ranks, feature names and policy against trusted inputs; reject contradictory or missing essential claims. Bound regeneration attempts and separately verify the deterministic replacement. The current verifier parses only the template sentence grammar. Extracting and binding claims from free prose through a constrained payload is the Mid-2 deliverable, not a current capability.

\textbf{Planned evaluation:} compare deterministic template, raw prompted Phi-3-mini and later adapted student on the same frozen cases. Score raw output before filtering and emitted output after filtering; report the four proposal metrics, contradictions, retries, fallback rate and whole-request latency. Participant tasks test whether prose improves interpretation beyond the template. QLoRA is scheduled for Iteration 4.
\subsection{Illustrative Design Example, Not a System Output}
This adapted concept Section 10.8 example uses hypothetical inputs: Hold probability 0.58, news polarity +0.31, social polarity -0.18 and RSI 38.2. Suppose divergence contributes +0.14 and RSI contributes -0.06 to the raw Hold score. A future explanation could say: ``The diagnostic class is Hold with 58\% confidence. News and social tone disagree. The recorded divergence supports Hold, while the RSI contribution opposes Hold. These are model effects; they do not establish what caused the price to move.'' The displayed policy must come from a separately supplied trusted policy result.

\section{Design Evolution}
The proposal's broader conversational pipeline remains the destination. The delivered slice adds complete evidence, independent text/JSON checking, a full profile and abstention. Isotonic failures and the earlier risk/sentiment blend are retained as historical comparisons. Generated explanations and divergence require separate integration/evaluation.

\section{Data Design}
Experiment source/digest and session jointly identify a case. Feature maps retain names, values and contributions. Candidate/profile digests bind verification to the current state. JSON files and session memory are the current storage; no relational database is implemented.
