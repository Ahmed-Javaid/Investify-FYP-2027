\chapter{Iteration 1: Implementation and Scientific Execution}
\section{Implemented Pipeline}
The pipeline validates versioned price/news snapshots, maps timestamps to exchange sessions, builds causal indicators and news aggregates, and trains/evaluates a fixed XGBoost configuration. Each decision record retains identity, probabilities, complete feature/SHAP maps, raw score, model version and input digests. The interface reads those records; the verifier and risk calculations execute live.

\section{Temporal Controls and Terminology}
The target spans five observed trading sessions. Purging excludes a training label whose actual endpoint reaches the evaluation start. The configured five-day embargo is an additional calendar-day gap: these are different rules. Replacing endpoint logic with calendar subtraction retained 18 invalid endpoints on the boundary fixture, while the corrected purge retained zero. The explanation of this distinction resolves the proposal/report terminology difference rather than treating the two terms as synonyms.

The no-purge arm retains an additional gap and may show no detectable performance inflation when only a few boundary outcomes are affected. The same-bar branch remains a lag ablation under the strict-prior join, not a guaranteed future leak. A centred window explicitly consumes future observations and is the permanent canary. The shuffled split breaks chronological evaluation. Each arm has a different fault geometry; not every deliberate variation must inflate the measured score.

\section{Calibration Diagnosis and Correction}
Independent isotonic maps force Buy because its pre-normalization minimum 0.5344828 exceeds Hold maximum 0.2500000 and Sell maximum 0.3139535. Normalizing by a common positive sum preserves that ordering. We retain those records as the diagnosed failure and construct a new global-temperature experiment from the same immutable model bytes, scaler, margins and input rows.

The single temperature is selected by negative log likelihood on the 181 earlier calibration outcomes; evaluation labels do not fit it. Its optimum reaches the configured upper bound of 1,000, making probabilities nearly uniform. Rank preservation removes the artificial all-Buy collapse, but does not add predictive information. The interface therefore exposes the diagnostic class and withholds a directional recommendation at insufficient confidence. Brier and classification metrics are reported together; the correction is not marketed as a profitable or well-calibrated predictor.

Native XGBoost replay reproduces the retained margins. TreeSHAP is recalculated for each newly selected raw class and checked for additivity. Both numeric and complete-guided explanations are checked on all newly generated records. The new model identity keeps the original classifier and calibration findings traceable.
\section{Complete Risk Profiling and Policy}
Five capacity factors use retained weights: horizon/buffer 0.30 each, income stability 0.20, dependants/age 0.10 each. Protective caps handle no buffer, short horizon and unstable income with thin reserves. Thirteen Grable--Lytton item scores produce $G\in[13,47]$ and normalized tolerance $T_r=(G-13)/34$. The risk allowance is $R=\min(C,T_r)$; the more restrictive capacity/tolerance tier is displayed.

The primary policy requires confidence $\tau(R)=0.80-0.30R$ for a directional suggestion. A higher allowance lowers that threshold but cannot change Buy into Sell or Sell into Buy. Weak evidence remains Observe; an existing Hold class remains non-directional. The old $C=wS+(1-w)T$ blend remains a historical baseline with explicit weighting, not the meaning of the current risk score. The published scoring is implemented; local paraphrases and the confidence policy require further suitability evaluation.

\section{Explanation Checking and Teacher Construction}
\label{sec:teacher-pilot}
The checker validates actual visible prose independently of JSON, including probabilities, values, feature names, attribution direction/rank, coverage and maintained meanings. Known contradictions fail before rate-based metrics can dilute them. Rejection preserves the failed report and triggers an independently checked deterministic fallback.

The teacher construction pilot contains six actual recorded explanations: four training and two validation exports. Factual JSON is assembled from the source record and is distinct from generated prose. Grouped dates keep test sessions outside the pilot. This establishes example construction and bounded checking, not trained-student efficacy or participant understanding. The larger prompted/student/teacher evaluation remains a later milestone.
