\subsection{Temporal Controls}

The target spans five observed trading sessions. Purging excludes a training label whose actual endpoint reaches the evaluation start. The configured five-day embargo is an additional calendar-day gap: these are different rules. Replacing endpoint logic with calendar subtraction retained 18 invalid endpoints on the boundary fixture, while the corrected purge retained zero. The explanation of this distinction resolves the proposal/report terminology difference rather than treating the two terms as synonyms.

The no-purge arm retains an additional gap and may show no detectable performance inflation when only a few boundary outcomes are affected. The same-bar branch remains a lag ablation under the strict-prior join, not a guaranteed future leak. A centred window explicitly consumes future observations and is the permanent canary. The shuffled split breaks chronological evaluation. Each arm has a different fault geometry; not every deliberate variation must inflate the measured score.

\subsection{Calibration Diagnosis and Abstention}

Independent isotonic maps force Buy because its pre-normalization minimum 0.5344828 exceeds Hold maximum 0.2500000 and Sell maximum 0.3139535. Normalizing by a common positive sum preserves that ordering. We retain those records as the diagnosed failure and construct a new global-temperature experiment from the same immutable model bytes, scaler, margins and input rows.

The single temperature is selected by negative log likelihood on the 181 earlier calibration outcomes; evaluation labels do not fit it. Its optimum reaches the configured upper bound of 1,000, making probabilities nearly uniform. Class order is preserved, but probabilities approach a constant one-third forecast. Its multiclass Brier score is about 0.6667, the constant $1/3$ forecast value $\sum_{k=1}^{3}(1/3-y_k)^2=2/3$. The observed value is slightly higher before rounding. There is no usable confidence signal, so the policy abstains. The interface therefore exposes the diagnostic class and withholds a directional recommendation at insufficient confidence. Brier and classification metrics are reported together; this is a no-signal diagnosis, not a usable forecast.

Native XGBoost replay reproduces the retained margins. TreeSHAP is recalculated for each newly selected raw class and checked for additivity. Both numeric and complete-guided explanations are checked on all newly generated records. The new experiment identity keeps the original classifier and calibration findings traceable.

\subsection{Complete Risk Profiling and Policy}

Financial ability and willingness to bear loss are different\cite{finra_risk_tolerance}; our policy keeps both scores. The tolerance instrument originates in Grable and Lytton\cite{grable_lytton_1999}, with the scoring reviewed through the later retrospective\cite{grable_retrospective_2015}. Five capacity factors use retained weights: horizon/buffer 0.30 each, income stability 0.20, dependants/age 0.10 each. Protective caps handle no buffer, short horizon and unstable income with thin reserves. Thirteen Grable--Lytton item scores produce $G\in[13,47]$ and normalized tolerance $T_r=(G-13)/34$. The risk allowance is $R=\min(C,T_r)$; the more restrictive capacity/tolerance tier is displayed.

The primary policy requires confidence $\tau(R)=0.80-0.30R$ for a directional suggestion. A higher allowance lowers that threshold but cannot change Buy into Sell or Sell into Buy. Weak evidence remains Observe; an existing Hold class remains non-directional. The old $C=wS+(1-w)T$ blend remains a historical baseline with explicit weighting, not the meaning of the current risk score. The published scoring is implemented; local paraphrases and the confidence policy require further suitability evaluation.
\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering\input{figures/mid1_risk_flow}\caption{Risk capacity/tolerance calculation and direction-preserving confidence policy.}\label{fig:risk-flow}\end{figure}

On real temperature records, even maximum allowance gives a 50\% threshold against confidence near 33\%; the profile never changes their Observe outcome. Controlled 0.70-confidence Buy/Sell fixtures show the intended policy behavior separately: higher allowance can clear the threshold without reversing direction or changing probabilities.
\subsection{Proposal Gate Outcomes}
\label{sec:gates}
\textbf{Gate A: partial, not fully closed.} The reference gate requires timing/session checks, fold-local scaling, both negative controls at chance and a firing mutation test. The saved one-session clean arm is PASS (0.3079 versus random 0.3314); the centred canary fires at both horizons. The five-session clean arm is BELOW\_CHANCE (0.2843 versus 0.3287), so it is not a literal chance-level pass for the whole gate. Original historical vendor availability is also unproven. Structural timing checks and a firing canary are supported; unconditional leakage-free closure is not.

\textbf{Gate B: not passed.} It requires macro-F1 and calibration improvement over the linear baseline under purged out-of-sample evaluation, with controls still at chance. The CV macro-F1 difference is only 0.0047 and clean prediction is below stratified random. The final isotonic classification lifts do not establish the joint CV calibration criterion; temperature reduces confidence to approximately one-third. The proposal's null-result path applies: retain the harness/verifier contribution and continue analysis without claiming useful prediction.
