\chapter{Testing and Early Validation}
\label{chap:testing}
\section{Testing Strategy}
Pair expected behavior with deliberate errors and retain exact logs. Software tests check consistency and failure handling; scientific evaluation checks prediction/calibration on declared populations. Neither is a participant study.
\section{Test Levels and Techniques}
Unit tests cover record invariants, formulas, omissions and malformed input. Module tests combine context/profile/template/checker. Streamlit AppTest exercises widgets and state; localhost HTTP checks exercise the portable service. Receipt and extraction guards check source/PDF packaging. Scientific fault arms and authored classifier probes have their own population and interpretation.

Appendix C lists input, expected behavior and named checks. The live demonstration emphasizes probability corruption, while fixture tests also cover values, signs, ranks, unknown features, missing claims, wrong policy and stale state.

\subsection{Social Results and Reference Construction}
The saved study acquired 200 MSFT posts, retaining 117 references and 83 exclusions. The chronological split is 93 development/24 held out; held-out classes are 7 positive, 15 neutral and 2 negative. CryptoBERT macro-F1 is 0.2564 with exact agreement 15/24, all predictions neutral. Cardiff macro-F1 is 0.6016 with exact agreement 16/24, positive recall 4/7 and negative recall 1/2.

References were constructed in one model-assisted pass declared as OpenAI/Codex GPT-6.1 Sol configuration, without an independently authenticated serving revision. No human annotation or inter-rater agreement was measured. Scores describe agreement with those references. Five separate authored CryptoBERT probes produce positive and negative outputs; the nominal neutral probe is negative. These diagnostic probes do not replace the held-out benchmark.
\section{Acceptance Testing / Validation}
The engineering acceptance path is case selection, evidence, complete profile, policy, checking and recovery. The transparency tests verify onboarding, captions and an Observe headline. Participant acceptance, independent external installation and formal supervisor review have not been observed.
\section{Edge and Failure Cases}
Missing/malformed records withhold replacement decisions. Incomplete profiles withhold scoring. Nonfinite values, unknown features, missing essential claims, conflicting prose/JSON and wrong policy fail. Changing the source/session/profile/text clears the verdict. Failed fallback is surfaced as an error. Real uniform-confidence cases remain Observe under both example profiles; controlled 0.70 fixtures exercise qualifying directional cases separately.
\section{Defects and Corrective Actions}
Corrections include payload-over-prose acceptance, omitted evidence, stale state, label-end purging, unavailable calibration labels and portable provenance paths. This revision aligns the Observe headline and explanation policy, adds onboarding/step disclosures and fixes an indentation error left in a UI test. Template and verifier patterns change together, while original teacher packets remain replayable.
\section{Current Validation Status}
The local checks establish the bounded software path. The final hosted matrix and delivery are identified in Appendix D once published. Predictor usefulness, generated prose, population-specific profiling and human acceptance are evaluated separately in the remaining plan.
