\chapter{Introduction}
\section{Project Overview}
Investify is an educational equity decision-support system. It combines technical indicators and financial-news sentiment, presents the evidence behind a model output, and checks explanations against that evidence. The prototype serves historical research cases; it does not connect to brokerage accounts or execute orders.
\section{Problem Statement}
An investor must interpret price indicators, news, uncertainty and personal risk constraints. A Buy/Hold/Sell label alone does not show which inputs influenced the result. Historical evaluations can also overstate performance when features or training outcomes become available after the decision time. Finally, fluent explanations can misstate numerical values or attribution directions. We address temporal evaluation and explanation consistency as explicit engineering problems.
\section{Objectives and Contributions}
Our implemented contributions are a leakage-audited evaluation pipeline and a record-grounded explanation-verification path. The former checks availability, chronological processing and deliberate faults; the latter exposes all available feature evidence, checks displayed and structured claims independently, and verifies recovery. Risk profiling and uncertainty gating connect these controls to the interface. News/social divergence remains a planned ablation rather than a completed result.
\section{Scope and Modules}
The full product targets the proposal's seven-company equity universe. The current research path is bounded to NVDA over 2018--2023, with a separate MSFT social-model comparison. The exposed prediction horizon is five trading sessions. Figure~\ref{fig:planned-architecture} distinguishes the six product modules and their current interfaces. Broader social integration, trained-student explanations and production serving remain later work.

\section{Stakeholders and Success Criteria}
The retail learner inspects evidence and uncertainty; the operator maintains data and runtime health; the supervisor assesses the implementation and the team defends its modules. Success is assessed through causal-alignment checks, deliberate-fault detection, held-out prediction/calibration results, explanation consistency, reproducible setup and later participant tasks. Software correctness and predictive usefulness are reported separately.
\section{Constraints, Privacy and Accessibility}
The historical demonstration uses versioned public market material and local profile answers. API credentials remain outside source control and report artifacts. Profile data is kept in the local session and is not sent to a brokerage. Text labels accompany colors, controls are keyboard accessible and evidence remains inspectable without a GPU. The interface withholds directional suggestions when predictive confidence is insufficient. Population-specific risk assumptions, data vintage and eventual user acceptance require further evaluation.
