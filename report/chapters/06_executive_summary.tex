\chapter*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Executive Summary}
Investify is an educational stock research prototype. It addresses historical information leakage and explanations that misstate a model's evidence. The delivered flow loads a saved case, exposes all technical/news values and SHAP, scores a complete risk profile and checks visible text independently of its structured claims. A rejected explanation retains its error report and produces a separately checked deterministic fallback.

The historical study covers NVDA during 2018--2023. FNSPID remains an earlier comparator; timestamp precision and coverage gaps led to a new Alpaca/Benzinga study. The clean five-session walk-forward macro-F1 is 0.2843, below the stratified-random 0.3287 comparator, and close to the linear 0.2796 result. The centred Bollinger canary detects deliberate future information. Six-fold averages and the final 186-case evaluation are reported separately.

Isotonic calibration improves Brier while emitting all Buy. The separately identified temperature fit reaches T = 1000, giving probabilities near one-third and Brier about 0.6667. This is a no-signal diagnosis. Even the least restrictive real profile threshold is 50\%, so all these cases remain Observe. Controlled 0.70-confidence fixtures demonstrate policy behavior without manufacturing a real prediction.

The report answers PF-01--PF-09 through contributions C1--C3, a defined future LLM layer, pinned models, evaluation targets, market mechanisms/formulas, transparent requirements and the live corruption/recovery PoC. The current explainer is deterministic; prompted explanations with constrained claims are scheduled for Mid-2, and QLoRA for Iteration 4.

Ahmed delivered temporal/harness and CI work, Rafay delivered interface/profile and sentiment work, and Soban delivered engine/SHAP and verification work. The equal talk allocation is 220 seconds each. The remaining plan covers broader data/divergence, useful prediction/calibration, service/NFRs and participant evaluation.
