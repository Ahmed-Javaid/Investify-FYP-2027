\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
Investify is an educational equity decision-support prototype that combines technical indicators, news sentiment and explanations checked against recorded evidence. Its verifier checks visible prose and JSON independently and supplies a deterministic fallback. A prototype risk questionnaire conditions a separate retrospective linear comparator.

A fresh NVDA experiment processes 1,509 price sessions and 6,768 revision-conservative news rows. The clean five-day model obtains macro-F1 0.2843 versus a stratified-random baseline of 0.3287. A deliberately centred indicator triggers the leakage canary. Both corrected calibration modes produce only Buy decisions on 186 evaluation sessions; improved Brier scores therefore do not establish useful classification.

Original CryptoBERT and Cardiff classifiers complete the same 24 frozen MSFT posts, scoring macro-F1 agreement of 0.2564 and 0.6016 against AI reference labels. The small, imbalanced reference has no independent human validation, and deployment selection remains open.

Recorded-model, calibration and native TreeSHAP replay pass. The interfaces expose evidence, failure cases and recorded comparisons. Six assistant-teacher explanations form a construction pilot; the planned Phi-3-mini student remains untrained. Broader generalisation, live LLM usefulness, agreed risk semantics, institutional completion and genuine team review remain outstanding.
