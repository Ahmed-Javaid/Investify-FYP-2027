\begin{frame}{Problem}


\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{4mm}

\item A weak signal needs confidence and a record of its inputs.
\item Historical leakage can inflate results before the user sees them.
\item Fluent explanations can invent a number or a market cause.
\end{itemize}
