- Complete interface failure/recovery and accessibility review with actual people, with recorded findings and fixes. Do not infer participant understanding from AppTest.
- Complete genuine member code defence, contribution reconciliation and the required remote CI evidence; retain a reproducible, source-bound handover.
- Obtain actual report/design/proposal-action review and a successful rehearsal. Required metadata and approvals must be genuine.
