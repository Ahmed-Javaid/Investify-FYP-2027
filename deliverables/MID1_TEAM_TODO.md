# Team tasks after technical completion

These tasks remain visible but are excluded from the technical Mid-1 completion percentage, as requested by the team.

- Read the report and verify the team's statements.
- Print the report and sign the required declaration/submission pages.
- Review scope, requirements/design and proposal responses with the supervisor.
- Each member studies their assigned code and completes an individual walkthrough.
- Complete the shared timed rehearsal using the three preparation packets.
- Check the physical projector/presentation setup and record any issues.
- Complete submission arrangements and any applicable data/model-use or participant permissions before the relevant activity.

The coordinator field is intentionally omitted from the report. Existing names, roll numbers, supervisor, Fall 2026 term and report date 11 October 2026 are retained. Actual student review, signatures and research quality are not asserted by technical completion.
